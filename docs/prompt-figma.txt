# Prompt para Figma AI

Cole o prompt abaixo no Figma AI (ou em qualquer gerador de layout):

---

Create a landing page design for a SaaS product called "Robô Vendedor" (Portuguese for "Sales Robot"). The product is an AI-powered sales assistant that works 24/7 via WhatsApp, answering customer questions and capturing leads automatically.

## Style
- Clean, modern SaaS aesthetic (similar to Vercel, Linear, Raycast landing pages)
- Dark theme (#0a0a0a background) or clean light theme (off-white) — choose what looks better
- Accent color: bright blue or violet (#3b82f6 or #7c3aed)
- Sans-serif typography (Inter or similar)
- Generous whitespace
- Smooth rounded corners on cards and buttons

## Layout Structure (top to bottom)

### 1. Navigation (fixed top)
- Logo on the left: "Robô Vendedor" or simple robot icon
- Navigation links: "Como funciona" (How it works) | "Contato" (Contact)
- No pricing in the nav

### 2. Hero Section
- Headline: "Seu robô vendedor 24 horas por dia" (Your sales robot 24/7)
- Subheadline: "IA que atende, tira dúvidas e captura leads enquanto você trabalha." (AI that serves, answers questions and captures leads while you work.)
- Two CTA buttons:
  - Primary: "Quero meu robô vendedor" (filled button)
  - Secondary: "Ver demonstração" (outlined button)
- Right side: A mockup showing a smartphone screen with a chat interface running
- The mockup should show a conversation between a customer and the AI robot

### 3. How It Works (3 steps)
- Title: "Como funciona"
- Three horizontal cards/columns:
  1. "1. Cadastre seus produtos" (Register your products) — icon: box/package
  2. "2. Compartilhe o link" (Share the link) — icon: link/share
  3. "3. IA atende e captura leads" (AI serves and captures leads) — icon: message/chat

### 4. Benefits Section
- Title: "Por que escolher?" (Why choose it?)
- Grid of 4 benefit cards with icons:
  - ⏰ "Atendimento 24h" — Never lose a sale due to delay
  - 🎯 "Lead qualificado" — Only qualified leads reach you
  - 🤖 "Respostas inteligentes" — AI trained on YOUR products
  - 📈 "Mais vendas" — Increase sales without more work

### 5. Live Demo / Chat Section
- Title: "Experimente agora mesmo" (Try it right now)
- Subtitle: "Este chat é seu robô funcionando. Teste!" (This chat is your robot working. Test it!)
- A live chat widget/box in the center of the page showing:
  - AI greeting message bubble: "Olá! 👋 Me conta qual seu negócio que eu te explico como posso ajudar."
  - Input field at the bottom: "Digite sua mensagem..." with a send button
  - A floating chat button visible on all sections

### 6. Target Audience Section
- Title: "Para quem é?" (Who is it for?)
- List of audience types with small icons:
  - Lojas físicas que vendem no WhatsApp (Physical stores selling on WhatsApp)
  - Profissionais autônomos (Freelancers: personal trainers, nutritionists)
  - Prestadores de serviço (Service providers)
  - Qualquer um que perde venda por demora (Anyone losing sales due to delay)

### 7. Final CTA Section
- Headline: "Quer levar isso pro seu negócio?" (Want to bring this to your business?)
- Large CTA button: "Quero meu robô vendedor →"
- Below: "Sem compromisso. Teste grátis." (No commitment. Free trial.)

### 8. Footer
- Copyright year
- Contact information
- Simple, minimal

## Key Design Notes
- A floating chat button should be visible in the bottom-right corner across ALL sections (like Intercom/Drift style)
- Use gradient accents subtly
- Keep it conversion-focused: each section leads to the CTA
- Mobile responsive layout (stack vertically on mobile)
- Use subtle animations suggestions (fade in on scroll)
- Portuguese language throughout
- No pricing information anywhere
